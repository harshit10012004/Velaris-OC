# Session Summary — Harshit Sharma — Spa-Francorchamps

## What We Looked At
We compared two clean GT3 laps at Spa and looked for the largest lap-time losses.

## What the Data Showed
The comparison lap was 0.700 seconds slower overall.

The biggest difference was at Pouhon, where the comparison lap entered the corner slower and lost more speed through the apex.

## What to Try Next
Brake slightly earlier for Pouhon and focus on carrying a steadier minimum speed through the corner.

> This is a driving technique, not a setup change.

## What to Watch For
Watch whether the car carries more speed through Pouhon without making the entry unstable.

## Next Step
Run five clean laps using the same braking reference and compare the results.
